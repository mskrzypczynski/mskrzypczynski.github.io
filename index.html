Chrome extensions

Free developer tools for Salesforce OmniStudio. No analytics, no tracking, nothing sent to anyone but your own org.

OmniScript Data Inspector

Live OmniScript data, structure and remote action log, in a DevTools panel.

OmniScript Replay

Record the OmniScript steps you keep repeating, then replay them the way a person would, in a DevTools panel. Experimental.

OmniStudio Finder

Find OmniScripts, Integration Procedures, DataRaptors and FlexCards in your Salesforce org. See every version, open the right one.

Source: omniscript-data-inspector (GPL-3.0 from 2.0) · omniscript-replay (GPL-3.0) · omnistudio-finder (GPL-3.0)
