OmniStudio Finder

Chrome extension · toolbar popup

On the Chrome Web StoreFree

Find any OmniStudio asset in your org and open the right version.

  • Search by name or paste a Type_SubType_Language key.
  • Every version, the active one marked.
  • Opens the right designer, core or managed package.

OmniScript Data Inspector

Chrome extension · DevTools panel

On the Chrome Web StoreFree

See what a running OmniScript holds, in a DevTools panel.

  • The live data, as you click through.
  • Why a field is hidden or Next is blocked.
  • Every call, failures flagged.

Privacy policies

What each tool reads, keeps and sends.

  • OmniStudio Finder Uses your session for the org in your tab. Keeps settings in your browser.
  • OmniScript Data Inspector Reads the page you inspect. Your data stays with you.
  • OmniScript Replay Keeps recordings in your browser.
