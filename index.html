OmniStudio Finder

Chrome extension · toolbar popup

On the Chrome Web StoreFree

Find any OmniScript, Integration Procedure, DataRaptor or FlexCard in the org of the tab you are on. See every version, open the right one.

  • Search as you type, or paste a Type_SubType_Language key straight from a log.
  • Every version as a chip, the active one in green; Enter opens it.
  • Opens the right designer for core OmniStudio or the Vlocity packages.

OmniScript Data Inspector

Chrome extension · DevTools panel

On the Chrome Web StoreFreePro coming

Live OmniScript data, structure and calls, in a DevTools panel next to the page you are testing.

  • The live data JSON, with what changed highlighted as you click through.
  • Every element joined to its data, with visibility rules you can read.
  • Every remote call, with errors flagged even inside an HTTP 200.

The free edition stays free. Pro will add the deeper debugging work on top: test calls, debug runs, comparisons and change history.

Details Privacy

How they are built

  • No trackingNo analytics, no server of my own. Your org's data goes to your org and nowhere else.
  • Least permissionOnly what a feature needs, and the privacy policy says exactly what that is.
  • Open sourceThe free editions are GPL-3.0, worth reading for anything that touches your session.
  • Read before writeReading is the default. Nothing changes your org without showing you what it will do first.

Privacy policies

Each tool has its own policy: what it reads, what it keeps in your browser, and where anything is sent.

  • OmniStudio Finder Uses your session for the org in your tab, to list and open its assets. Keeps its settings in your browser.
  • OmniScript Data Inspector Reads the page you inspect in DevTools. Keeps panel preferences in your browser.
  • OmniScript Replay Records and replays steps on the page. Keeps recordings in your browser.
