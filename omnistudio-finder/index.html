OmniStudio Finder

Find OmniScripts, Integration Procedures, Data Mappers and FlexCards in your Salesforce org, see every version and open the right one.

Chrome Web Store Source on GitHub

What it does

  • Searches the org you are on. Filter by name, type, subtype, language or author as you type.
  • Takes the key you already have. Paste Type_SubType_Language from a log and that script comes first.
  • Shows every version. One chip per version, the active one in green.
  • Opens the right designer. Enter opens the active version; click a chip for another. Core and Vlocity managed packages are both handled.

Privacy

No server, no analytics, nothing sent anywhere. Your session is used to read your own org and is never stored. Read the privacy policy.
